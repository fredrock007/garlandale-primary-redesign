OUR SCHOOL

An independent redesign with
Garlandale at its centre.

Garlandale Primary School serves families in Athlone and is presented here through an independent website redesign concept. The goal of this concept is to show how the school’s story, information and achievements could be organised more beautifully and more clearly online.

This concept draws visual inspiration from the school’s crest and the navy-and-grey uniform seen on the school’s public Facebook presence. It also responds to recent published stories about the school’s investment in learning spaces, including its renovated library and digital-learning improvements.

A future production website could present the school with warmth and confidence while helping parents quickly find key information, such as admissions guidance, school notices, events, documents and contact details.

Identity

Inspired by the school itself.

The concept uses navy, white and grey to reflect Garlandale’s observed school colours and gives the crest visual prominence.

Clarity

Information that is easier to find.

A better information structure helps families find news, documents, admissions information and school dates more quickly.

Trust

A cleaner, more professional first impression.

A polished website can reflect care, organisation and the positive work happening inside the school.
